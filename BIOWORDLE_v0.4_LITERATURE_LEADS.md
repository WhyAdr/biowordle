# v0.4 candidate biological literature leads

These are context-specific **abstract-level research leads**, not approved game hints or a completed full-paper review. Match every future claim to the full study and its methods, organism, substrate form and limitations. Database structure matches do not prove biological activity. The 25 other candidate rows already have leads in `BIOWORDLE_v0.4_CANDIDATE_MANIFEST.json`; this ledger covers its remaining 17 and intentionally does not modify the benchmark input manifest.
