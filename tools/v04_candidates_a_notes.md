# v0.4 proposal: aromatic, plant, and therapeutic candidates

Eighteen proposed new records; all remain `pending`, with no enabled production eligibility. Candidate names were selected to add comparative sets and organism-specific teaching opportunities. They are proposals, not a determination that each is suitable for the final release.

On 2026-11-14, PubChem PUG REST supplied CID, formula, isomeric SMILES and standard InChIKey; EBI OLS4 supplied the matching ChEBI ontology identifier and its annotated SMILES, formula and standard InChIKey. The manifest records the PubChem CID and ChEBI identifiers, properties and lookup URLs for every candidate. All 18 selected neutral molecular graphs are single-component, uncharged, with zero RDKit potential stereogenic elements; standard keys and independently parsed RDKit canonical isomeric graph representations match between the two sources. This is a database comparison, not independent experimental re-determination of molecular identity. No tautomer canonicalization was applied and no claim is made that the represented neutral species dominates at biological pH. The molecule, not a formulation salt, is the comparison unit.

`chem_engine.validate_chemle_manifest` passed on all 78 existing entries plus the 18 pending records: its usual exact identifiers, full keys and largest-fragment uncharged parent duplication checks found no conflict. A copy with in-memory Learn eligibility and verified labels also passed the same structural validator for research-only preview, without changing the pending source manifest. This does not establish biological verification, exhaustive tautomer or near-isomer distinctness, or permission for production inclusion. The combined 42 additions must be validated together.

Each candidate includes one narrow, context-specific experimental study under `v04_review.biological_evidence`; these are candidate teaching leads, not claims that all organisms share that pathway or that the game hint has been scientifically approved. The supplied production hint fields are deliberately generic placeholders, not final teaching text. A curator must review the referenced full studies, check claim wording and select educational hints with citation mapping before accepting any candidate. Stereochemical, tautomer and protonation policies must be reviewed against the intended species and supplied biological context.

The initially considered metformin was replaced by dapsone: RDKit flagged an unspecified potentially stereogenic amidine bond in the neutral PubChem graph, whereas the current validator cannot express this as an unspecified non-racemate representation. This is a schema/policy limitation and does not itself prove the reference is invalid. No stereochemical or validator gate was weakened.
